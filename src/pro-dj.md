# Playing A³: DJ knowledge

What a DJ or live act needs to know to play A³: the booth, preparing and
structuring a set, moving sound in 3D, and how A³ relates to the gear a DJ
already knows (CDJ-3000, Pro DJ Link, rekordbox, Serato, Traktor, DJM mixers).

These pages are written and kept in the
[a3-pro-dj](https://github.com/a3-audio/a3-pro-dj) repository and included here
when the docs are built. For the devices themselves, see the
[Welcome](user/user.md) page.
